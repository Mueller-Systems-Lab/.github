# Mueller Systems Lab brand authority

The canonical visual reference for Mueller Systems Lab is the owner-supplied artwork confirmed on 2026-08-28.

## Non-negotiable visual elements

- Deep navy background.
- Metallic silver `M` above a metallic silver `S`.
- Cyan segmented circular system ring.
- Cyan measurement / pulse line with round terminals.
- Ground symbol centered below the `S`.
- Wordmark: `MÜLLER` above `SYSTEMS LAB`.
- Product identities remain independent; the Mueller Systems Lab mark is an ownership / provenance endorsement.

## Drift prevention

Do not generate a replacement logo from a text prompt. Do not invent new monogram geometry, signal paths, symbols, colors or typography and present them as canonical branding.

When a new size or layout is needed, derive it from the confirmed reference and preserve the mark geometry and visual language. A banner may change composition to fit a wide canvas, but the mark itself must remain visually consistent with the reference.

## Repository use

Repositories with medium branding may show `docs/brand/mueller-systems-lab-icon.svg`. Repositories with light branding should generally keep only the textual ownership endorsement so that product identity remains primary.
